## What's new

### Settings
- Settings are regrouped into clearer categories. The old Miscellaneous page is gone and a new Player category holds playback behaviour, player display options, lyrics and Canvas blur.
- Smart version alignment moved to Playback.
- App icon, Listen Together tab and separate Search tab moved to Appearance.
- Reduce animation and Reduce dynamic blur moved to Performance.
- Advanced now only holds Nerd stats.

### Player
- The player dismiss chevron is hidden when Hide song status is on.

### Spotify Canvas
- With live blur off, lyrics and queue panels now use the default cover and a blurred artwork backdrop.
- Panel blur is applied instantly when lyrics or the queue opens.

### Fixes and polish
- The legacy mesh backdrop now opens with the cover colours right away, without fading in from fallback colours.
- The mesh snaps to the first real artwork palette, and the palette is cached for faster opening.

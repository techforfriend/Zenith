## What's new

### Player and queue
- Redesigned player header with a back chevron that closes the player and a two-line "Playing from" label.
- New Automix toggle next to AutoPlay in the queue mode row, with a frosted info popup on long press.
- Queue mode buttons are fully rounded with larger icons.
- The mini player now uses the same play and pause icons as the main player.
- Smooth fade-out when pausing playback.
- The audio output icon is tinted when routed to an external device.

### Spotify Canvas
- Optional live blurred Canvas behind lyrics and queue, for all clips.
- Smoother, blink-free transitions when opening or closing the player, lyrics and queue.
- The cover now flies smoothly between the mini player and the full player.
- Apple Music Canvas panels use the legacy mesh backdrop.

### Lyrics
- Manual lyrics search with an alternative title and artist, remembered per track.
- The globe button now opens a menu with romanize and lyrics provider options, shared across portrait, landscape and desktop.
- The lyrics provider is shown as a dim footer at the end of the lyrics.
- Lyrics buttons now use real Liquid Glass with their own settings.
- Smoother vocal sweep fade for unsung lyrics.

### Library and Home
- Reorder songs in on-device Spotify playlists.
- Playlist pages use a full-bleed hero and a single action row.
- Made for You mix pages show a centered mix title.
- Recommendation shelves now appear at the end of album pages.
- The Home Speed Dial and Top Picks now vary on every app launch.

### Settings
- New setting to auto-pause playback when the media volume hits zero.
- A rejected Discord token is now shown instead of being retried silently.

### Fixes and polish
- Fixed cover flashes, blinking and clipping during player transitions.
- Fixed the player chevron being clipped or misplaced in the header.
- Removed the audible cut when audio quality switches mid-track.
- Fixed the Automix marker staying on the seek bar after Automix is turned off.
- The legacy mesh backdrop now animates slower and continuously, with colours closer to the artwork.
- Removed the page zoom-out effect behind the player.
- Removed dead code and unused imports.
